Show vs. Tell Playground — one beat, five distances

Back on the cheap rail. Gravity and the Controller install standing state; this is Gesture Playground's shape — select a beat, generate a bounded workup, keep what's useful, commit one turn. What's new is the control: a continuum from compress / explain to dramatize / embody with a tradeoff readout under it. It is not a morality slider. Telling is a tool; the UI names what each position buys and what it costs, and never labels an end bad writing.

It's interactive: in §1, open the playground from Jill's chip or Widgets, drag the beat along the continuum and watch the readout re-weigh, generate the workup, then keep variants — each kept variant can ride as prose or as direction only, and the payload strip counts characters against a hard ceiling. Commit lands a chip that re-opens the exact draft.

The one-shot rail, live

Workshop thread + composerInteractivethread-artifact lifecycle, end to end
What this frame decides
  • Nothing new on the rail. This widget reuses Sprint 01 wholesale — one-shot thread-artifact, chip, clone-and-recommit. The novelty is entirely in the pre-commit surface, which is the cheapest possible way to add a craft control.
  • Comparison is the product. Auto-commit is deliberately out of scope: variations are only valuable because the writer sets them beside each other before choosing. A persona may recommend and prefill; it never picks.
  • It never touches editor text. No insert, no silent replace. Commit hands the room a directive and the variants the writer kept — the persona still writes the prose.
  • Jill's chip carries a diagnosis, not a verdict — "this line buys a year in nine words" is the same vocabulary the panel uses, so the recommendation and the control teach one model.
  • Clone-and-recommit, exactly as Spread 01: the chip re-hydrates the full draft — continuum position, channels, kept variants, carry modes — and committing again creates a new turn at the head.
02

The continuum, and why it isn't a slider of virtue

same beat, opposite ends — the readout re-weighs, it doesn't improve

Position 1 — State itthe told end, on purpose
Position 5 — Inhabitfull scene time
Decisions
  • Five named positions, not a percentage. State it · Summarize · Hinge · Evidence · Inhabit — teachable, diffable, and speakable in a thread marker. Same conviction as the Controller's discrete levers.
  • The readout is the anti-morality device. Seven dimensions, one accent colour, no green-good/red-bad: moving right trades speed and fact clarity for intimacy, emphasis and scene time. Nothing gets a score.
  • Two dimensions deliberately break monotonicity — ambiguity and reader work peak at Evidence, not at Inhabit. Full dramatization can over-explain; pure evidence is the most open position. That's a craft claim the readout is allowed to make.
  • "Must survive" is the invariant, not a style note: it's the fact, emotion, or turn every variation has to carry, and it's the first line of the committed payload.
  • POV is a constraint, not a channel. Close third on him means interiority can only be his inference about her. The panel says so and the generation is told so — otherwise "show more interiority" quietly breaks the POV.
  • Every control here is deterministic and free. Position, channels, budget, readout: instant. The single model seam is Generate the workup.
03

The workup

one generation · grouped alternatives · a craft note per variant · multi-select

After the model callHinge · 2 kept, one as direction only
Decisions
  • Grouped by kind, not ranked. Told cleanly / shown as evidence / shown from inside / mixed. No "best" badge, no ordering that implies improvement — the groups are the taxonomy the lesson teaches.
  • Every variant carries its tradeoff in its own words — a gains/costs line, plus a word count so the length budget is visible rather than theoretical. This is what makes the widget a teaching surface instead of a rewrite button.
  • Carry mode answers the promotion question, live. Each kept variant rides as a prose variant (the exact words, quoted) or as a direction only (the abstraction — around half the characters, and reusable on the next beat). Both, under one ceiling — the writer chooses per variant.
  • A hard size ceiling, shown as you spend it. The payload strip counts characters against 600 and turns red past it. Keeping seven variants as prose is possible and expensive; the UI makes the cost legible before the commit, not after.
  • The workup is thrown away. Seven variants, notes, readout, channels — none of it rides. What commits is the beat, the position, the invariant, the kept items, and an optional note.
  • The mixed group exists because it's usually the answer — tell the bridge, show the fulcrum. Its craft note says so, and then says to distrust it once.

Five positions map onto the Controller's three, never the reverse — the local surface may be finer-grained than the standing one, because it's arguing about one sentence rather than a whole passage. The ADR should fix this table, not leave each surface to invent its own words.

Boundaries this keeps
  • Lifetimes stay different on purpose. A local experiment must not quietly become a standing bias, and a standing bias must not be re-litigated per selection. Two rails, one vocabulary.
  • The Learner launches, never commits. A drill can prefill the playground — beat, invariant, target position — and stop there. Committing is always the writer's move.
  • If both are live, they don't fight. The Controller's standing lever is a default for prose it has no local instruction about; a committed playground artifact is a local instruction about one beat, and rides one turn. Local wins for that beat, and only that beat.
  • "Promote to standing" is a tempting fourth surface — a kept direction becoming a Controller shift. Explicitly out of the first slice; note it as the obvious next ask.

Invariants
  • Never rewrites the writer's text. No insert, no replace, no "apply to editor" in the first slice — the widget produces directions, the room produces prose.
  • Bounded payload, always. A hard ceiling with a visible counter; direction-only mode exists so a writer who keeps five variants still fits.
  • Config, not just output, persists — the full draft by stable id in WorkshopSessionService, so the chip re-opens authoring state rather than a summary.
  • New reserved frame registers with the neutralizer in the same change it ships.
  • Never a knob on the participant. Show/tell is a property of the work — no persona identity, lens, mode, or behavior is touched.
  • Core stays host-agnostic; only the composer mount touches the extension.

Promotion questions, as staked here: commit carries both prose variants and abstract directions, chosen per variant under one visible ceiling; context is the surrounding passage plus an explicit POV constraint and an invariant line — enough to preserve meaning, deliberately short of a rewrite tool; and the continuum vocabulary is pinned to the Controller's narrative handling chapter by the table in §4. Still open: whether a kept direction may ever be promoted to standing — which would need the Controller's shift marker, not this rail.
