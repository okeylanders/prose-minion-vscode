7Summary & movements

ReadSections: measured

Bored on the riverbank, Alice follows a White Rabbit with a watch down a rabbit-hole. The fall is long enough to be dull. She lands in a hall of locked doors, finds a key to a door too small, and starts changing size to reach a garden she can see but cannot enter.

I · ¶1–4 The bank

Boredom tips into pursuit.

¶4“In another moment down went Alice after it”✓ verbatim
II · ¶5–10 The well

A fall slow enough for talking to herself.

¶5“The rabbit-hole went straight on like a tunnel for some way”✓
III · ¶11–14 The hall

Locked doors, a key, a garden glimpsed.

¶11“Alice was not a bit hurt”✓
IV · ¶15–24 Drink me, eat me

The trial becomes size, not courage.

¶15“There seemed to be no use in waiting by the little door”✓

Where it strains: the landing could close II or open III. The boundary is drawn at ¶11 because the fall ends inside ¶10. Sections come from code only: Markdown headings, scene breaks (*** · #) and chapter lines. This chapter has none, so the ribbon stays in paragraphs.
