# Lightbox

## Boards

Open Lightbox from the Assistant sidebar or with **Prose Minion: Open Lightbox**. To start from the editor, use **Open Selection in Lightbox** for a passage or **Open File in Lightbox** for the whole chapter. The file command also appears in the Explorer. With a board open, you can choose a source from the project.

The board measures the text locally. The paragraph ribbon shows rhythm and dialogue. Hover over a repeated phrase, word or style flag to light up the paragraphs that contain it. Pin the item to inspect the sentences behind its count.

In a single-folder workspace, boards save automatically to `prose-minion/boards/`. Use the Boards menu to rename the board, board another chapter, reopen a prior board, or browse boards across chapters. A reopened board shows the chapter as it was when measured. **Read the current text** starts a board for the changed chapter. With no workspace folder or several roots, boards last for the session.

## Readings

Choose lenses on the Lenses rail. Run AI analyses reads the chapter with the chosen lenses. The estimate shows the calls and approximate cost. Readings use `proseMinion.lightboxModel`, which follows the assistant model until you pick a Lightbox model. You can run one call, re-analyze a tile, stop a run, or use Finish for the remaining calls. A previous reading stays visible until a usable replacement arrives.

The lenses cover the headline, theme and motif, tension arc, lexical gravity, palette, characters, voice, subtext, genre neighborhood, lineage, sparks, stock and signature phrasing, rhythm and handoffs, the numbers, template and freshness, pressure, artifacts, summary and movements, and craft steering. Opinion weighs the readings already on the board. Claims with chapter quotes are checked against the source.

Use **Bring to Workshop** on an eligible reading to stage the claim and checked quotes above the composer. You choose whether to send them. **Build as a lens** takes a lexical field into Lexical Gravity. You can also set sparks aside on the board.
