## Lens: motifs (key `"motifs"`)

What recurs: an image, an action, an object or a phrasing that comes back and gathers meaning as it does.

{"items":[{"title":"...","gist":"...","claim":"...","terms":["..."],"evidence":[{"para":2,"quote":"..."},{"para":5,"quote":"..."}]}],"friction":"..."}

- `items`: 0–4 motifs, the most present first. Zero is a valid answer when nothing recurs.
- A motif recurs, so each needs 2–3 quotes from at least two different paragraphs. A motif the host finds in only one paragraph is withheld.
- `title`: at most 80 characters, a name for the motif ("Going down"), not a verdict.
- `gist`: one line, at most 160 characters: how the motif moves through the chapter.
- `claim`: at most 400 characters: what the recurrence does, in the chapter's terms.
- `terms`: 1–5 words or short phrases that carry the motif, exactly as they stand. The host counts them.
- `friction`: where the pattern may be the reading's rather than the chapter's: an ordinary word doing ordinary work, a paragraph that breaks the pattern, a coincidence the reading enjoys.
