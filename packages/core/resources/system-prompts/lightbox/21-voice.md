## Lens: voice (key `"voice"`)

How the chapter is told.

{"pov":"...","tense":"...","distance":"...","register":"...","music":"...","claim":"...","evidence":[{"para":2,"quote":"..."}],"friction":"..."}

- `pov`, `tense`, `distance`, `register`, `music`: one line each, at most 160 characters.
  - `pov`: whose eyes and mind the telling uses, and how consistently.
  - `tense`: the tense of the narration.
  - `distance`: how close the narration sits to the point-of-view character, and where that changes.
  - `register`: the kind of language: plain, formal, wry, lyrical, clipped.
  - `music`: the rhythm the sentences make. Do not give numbers: the host sets its measured words per sentence beside this line.
- `claim`: at most 400 characters: what the voice does across the chapter. `evidence`: 1–3 quotes.
- Describe the voice; do not compare it with writers to emulate. A comparison describes a neighborhood, never a target.
- `friction`: where the description strains: a passage in another register, a narrator who is harder to place than the summary says.
