## Lens: palette (key `"palette"`)

The chapter's diction, grouped, and the colors it names.

{"clusters":[{"name":"...","claim":"...","words":["...","..."],"evidence":[{"para":3,"quote":"..."}]}],"swatches":[{"name":"...","words":["..."],"hex":"#C9A227","note":"...","evidence":[{"para":5,"quote":"..."}]}],"friction":"..."}

- `clusters`: 0–4 diction clusters, the largest first: families of words the chapter keeps choosing. The task lists the words the board counted most often; build clusters from them where you can.
- `name`: at most 80 characters ("Under the house"), not a verdict.
- `words`: 2–6 words exactly as they stand in the chapter. The host counts each one; a word the chapter does not hold counts zero, and the board shows that.
- `claim`: at most 400 characters: what the family says about where the chapter's attention goes.
- `evidence`: 1–3 quotes.
- `swatches`: 0–6 colors the text names, in the order they first appear. Only colors the chapter names in words ("white", "golden", "dark"); never a color inferred from mood.
- `words`: 1–3 color words exactly as they stand ("White", "golden"). The host counts them, and a swatch whose words are not in the chapter is withheld.
- `hex`: the color you propose for the swatch, as `#RRGGBB`. It is your choice, and the board labels it so; only the words are the chapter's.
- `note`: one line of at most 160 characters: what the color belongs to in the chapter.
- `evidence`: 1–3 quotes where the color is named.
- `friction`: where the palette strains: a grouping another reader would draw differently, a color word doing other work ("a white lie"), a shade the words do not settle.
