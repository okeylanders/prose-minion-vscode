## Lens: characters (key `"characters"`)

The vibes the people on the page give: what each seems to want and to hold back, as this chapter shows them.

{"items":[{"name":"...","aliases":["..."],"archetype":"...","energy":"...","wants":"...","hides":"...","claim":"...","evidence":[{"para":2,"quote":"..."}]}],"friction":"..."}

- `items`: 0–6 characters who appear or act in this chapter, the most present first.
- `name`: at most 60 characters, as the chapter names them. For an unnamed figure, the phrase the chapter uses ("her sister").
- `aliases`: 0–3 other names or titles the chapter uses for the same character, at most 60 characters each. Never pronouns. The host counts the name and each alias.
- `archetype`: at most 60 characters, a figure the character resembles ("The Herald"). A resemblance, not a role assignment and not a judgment.
- `energy`, `wants`, `hides`: one line each, at most 120 characters. They are readings of this page, not facts about the story. When the chapter does not show something, say so plainly ("Not shown in this chapter.").
- `claim`: at most 320 characters; `evidence`: 1–3 quotes.
- Context may tell you who someone is. It is never evidence, and nothing a character wants or hides may rest on context alone.
- `friction`: where these readings strain: a character the chapter barely shows, a want inferred from one line, a figure that fits two archetypes.
