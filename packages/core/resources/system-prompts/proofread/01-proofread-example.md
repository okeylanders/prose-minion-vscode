# Worked Example

The passage below adapts the opening of Lewis Carroll's *Alice's Adventures
in Wonderland* (1865, public domain), with errors added for this example.

## Passage

So she was considering in her own mind (as well as she could, for the hot day
made her feel very sleepy and stupid), weather the pleasure of making a
daisy-chain would be worth the trouble of getting up and picking the daisies,
when suddenly a White Rabbit with pink eyes runs close by her. There was
nothing so very remarkable in that; nor did Alice think it so very much out of
the the way to hear the Rabbit say to itself, “Oh dear! Oh dear! I shall be
late!”
