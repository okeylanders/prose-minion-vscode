## How the captures were made

Headless Chromium loads `dist/webview.js` with a stubbed `acquireVsCodeApi`. The harness posts `MODEL_DATA` (Claude Sonnet 5.5, 1M context) and `WORKSHOP_SESSION_STATE` (an open conversation with five standing files totalling 37,342 words). The reply carries `cachedTokens` 118,842, and `contextBudget` has a 30-minute `estimatedCacheExpiresAt`, `requestedMaxOutputTokens` 50,000 (hence 950K usable), and a matching `cacheRequestModelId`. The picker was driven with `workshop_context_catalog`, and settings with `SETTINGS_DATA` (`claudeCacheTtl: '1h'`) plus `OPEN_SETTINGS`. Fonts are Inter and JetBrains Mono from the webview's font stacks. The project names and the conversation are demo content.
