# v2.7.0 Marketplace README and Workshop tour additions

**Date:** 2026-10-03 01:15 UTC
**Branch:** `release/v2.7.0`, on top of `2d1cdd6` (release preparation)
**Requested by:** Okey, who supplied four Mac screenshots (Fricative entry, Dictionary switch, Craft Steering card, Workshop Tools picker)

## What changed

- **README:** two composite images in the house style of `screenshots/readme/`: `craft-steering.png` (sidebar + Workshop pickers) and `dictionary-topic-lexicon.png` (switch + entry). Both appear under "What's new in v2.7.0", with a short paragraph for each feature. The Dictionary section now mentions the switch.
- **Workshop tour:** two new `new` pages follow the Rewind page: *New: Craft Steering* and *New: Topic & Related Lexicon*. The tour is now nine pages. `WORKSHOP_STARTUP_NOTICE_VERSION` moves `v4` → `v5`. Neither version has shipped, so Marketplace users go from `v3` to `v5` as before, and any development machine that dismissed `v4` sees the tour again.
- **New notice shots** in `apps/vscode-extension/assets/workshop-notices/`: `tools-craft-steering`, `dictionary-topic-switch`, `dictionary-topic-entry`.
- **Changelogs:** user changelog notes the tour's return. The detailed changelog's notice line now says `v5`.

## How the images were made

- The three notice shots and the README's sidebar-picker panel are **2× captures of the real webview bundle**. A headless Chromium harness loads `dist/webview.js` with a stubbed `acquireVsCodeApi`, and the WorkshopApp test fixture supplies the session. Fonts are Inter and JetBrains Mono, both in the webview's own font stacks.
- `dictionary-topic-entry` reproduces the opening of Okey's real *fricative* output, posted through `DICTIONARY_RESULT`.
- The README's Workshop picker, Dictionary input, and entry panels are Okey's own screenshots.
- The README entry crop stops after the first topic. In Okey's *fricative* output, two second-topic book suggestions could not be confirmed as real books: "Sound Writing: The Art of Descriptive Audio" (Robert R. Haines), and "The Language of Film" attributed to Robert Stam. The first topic's books (Ladefoged & Johnson; Chomsky & Halle) are real.
- The feature-boundary guard reserves the title-case widget name. The Craft Steering page therefore says "creative variations" in lower case, as the 08c53f1 fix did.
