## How the captures were made

The method is v2.14.0's; see [that record](20261116-2300-release-v2.14.0-docs-tour-and-artwork.md). The scratch scripts were not committed.

- **Harness:** headless Chromium loads this tree's `dist/webview.js` as the Workshop surface. A stand-in for `acquireVsCodeApi` records what the tab posts and answers it.
- **Rooms:** `workshop_session_state` snapshots of made-up rooms about *Alice* (public domain).
  - **Context:** a `contextBudget` with a measured total and a breakdown whose piece names are the host's own (`WORKSHOP_CONTEXT_TALLY_PIECES`, the system-prompt section names, "Files (2)"). The numbers are demo figures.
  - **Earlier turns:** a window of four turns with `truncatedTurns: 212`, and three bookmarks, two with `inWindow: false`. Captured at 1060 px.
  - **`@`:** `@chap` typed into the composer; the host answers `workshop_request_context_catalog` with ten files, then answers the pick's `workshop_attach_message_resources` with a snapshot holding the pending attachment.
  - **Memory tab:** the host answers `workshop_memory_list` with an estimate for 46 memories at the Standard list size.
  - **Help and lens:** `help_search`, `help_read` and `lens_read` capability turns, with the summaries the host writes for them, and replies written for the demo. The lens is the built-in Photography lens, described from its own text.
- **No model was called.** The model name and every reply are demo content.
- **Captures:** 2× crops by element box, 0 page errors, each looked at before use. Composites are 1×, 1252 px wide.
- **The tour pages** were rendered from the rebuilt bundle at 1240×900 with the real shots: every image loads, no page scrolls, and each call-out sits on its control.
